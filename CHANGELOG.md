# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Dash momentum now carries into a slide. Landing into a slide from an air dash, or crouching out of a ground dash, opens the slide at your current speed instead of slowing you down.

## 1.1.0

- Faster slides: new **Fast slide finish** option (on by default). A slide stays faster than a sprint for its whole length, then drops out quickly, instead of crawling at crouch speed for its last couple of seconds. Same distance, about 20% faster on average.
- New defaults: slide speed 140%, locked slide direction, slides in any direction and on landing, 300% dash, stronger air steering, Diamonds stamina bar above the XP bar that hides when full, quieter sounds.
- The per-slide log line only appears with Diagnostics on.

## 1.0.0

- First release: sprint, slide, dash, air jumps, air control, mantle and sounds in one mod.
